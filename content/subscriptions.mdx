title: Subscriptions
description: Charge on a schedule. Each period is an invoice your subscriber pays on-chain, with reminders, a grace period and dunning.

A subscription bills one subscriber on one plan, every period. Nothing is charged automatically:
Nowgate holds no subscriber's funds. Each period it issues an **invoice**, emails it, and your
subscriber pays it on-chain like any payment, in the coin they choose. Reminders, a grace period
and dunning take the place of retried card charges. The [API reference](/api/subscriptions) has
every field.

```bash
curl https://api.nowgate.io/v1/plans \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $NOWGATE_PAYMENTS_KEY" \
  -H "Idempotency-Key: $(uuidgen)" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"name":"Coffee club","price_amount":"18","price_currency":"USD","interval":"month","trial_days":14}'

curl https://api.nowgate.io/v1/subscriptions \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $NOWGATE_PAYMENTS_KEY" \
  -H "Idempotency-Key: $(uuidgen)" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"plan":"plan_...","subscriber":{"email":"ana@example.com","locale":"es"}}'
```

## Plans

A plan is a price and how often it's paid: `day`, `week`, `month` or `year`, times
`interval_count`, from a day up to a year.

- **A price in a currency** (`price_amount` and `price_currency`, USD or EUR) is paid in any coin
  you accept, at the rate when the subscriber pays, as a payment priced in a currency is. Limit the
  coins with `assets`.
- **A price in a coin** (`amount`, `network` and `asset`) is paid in that coin only.
- **`trial_days`** gives new subscriptions a free trial, 0 to 365 days.
- **The price and the period never change.** A new price is a new plan: move subscriptions to it
  with a plan change. You can change a plan's name, description, trial, coins, markup and fee mode,
  and archive it: an archived plan takes no new subscriptions, and its subscriptions carry on.

## Subscriptions and their statuses

| Status       | Meaning                                                                         | Give access? |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------ |
| `trialing`   | In its free trial. The first invoice is due when it ends.                       | Yes          |
| `incomplete` | Made without a trial: its first invoice is due at once.                         | No           |
| `active`     | The current period is paid.                                                     | Yes          |
| `past_due`   | The period's invoice is unpaid past its due date, within the grace period.      | Yes          |
| `unpaid`     | The grace period is over. The invoice can still be paid until dunning ends.     | No           |
| `canceled`   | Ended, with `cancel_reason`: `merchant`, `subscriber`, `unpaid` or `incomplete`. | No           |

Access is your call: Nowgate tells you the status, and the column above is what we suggest.

- **`subscriber_url`** opens the subscriber's own page: their plan and its status, the invoice to
  pay, past invoices with their transactions, cancellation at the end of the period and the
  language of their emails. It's a link, not an account: replace it with
  `POST /v1/subscriptions/{id}/rotate-link` if it went astray.
- **`external_id`** is your own reference, unique among your subscriptions; find a subscription by
  it, or by the subscriber's `email`.
- **At most 1,000 new subscriptions a day**, so a leaked key can't make Nowgate email thousands of
  addresses: more is refused with `429 subscription_limit` and `Retry-After`.

## Invoices

One invoice at a time: a subscriber who falls behind owes one period, never several.

- **Issued** 7 days before it's due, or a quarter of the period before for periods under 28 days,
  and **due** when its period starts. A subscription without a trial gets its first invoice at once.
- **Paid by payments.** The invoice's `checkout_url` lets the subscriber pick a coin; each attempt is
  an ordinary payment for what remains, with `invoice` and `subscription` set. A partial payment
  counts, and the next attempt asks only for the rest. More than due marks it `overpaid`.
- **Statuses:** `open`, `paid` or `void` (`void_reason`: `canceled`, `unpaid` or `replaced`). An
  invoice's payments send no receipt: the subscriber gets the invoice's own paid email.

## Reminders, grace and dunning

| When                         | The subscription   | Email to the subscriber                  |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------ | ---------------------------------------- |
| The invoice is issued        |                    | The invoice, with its pay link           |
| 3 days and 1 day before due  |                    | A reminder                               |
| The due date, unpaid         | `past_due`         | Overdue                                  |
| Grace ends (3 days), unpaid  | `unpaid`           | Paused: paying starts it again           |
| 3 days before dunning ends   |                    | Final notice                             |
| Dunning ends (14 days)       | `canceled`         | Canceled, and the invoice is void        |

**Paying at any point before cancellation recovers the subscription.** Within the grace period the
period keeps its dates; after it, a new period starts at the payment. Change the grace period,
dunning and reminders in the dashboard's subscription settings; invoices issued after a change take
it.

## Emails

Nowgate emails subscribers in your brand: your name ("Lumen Coffee via Nowgate"), logo and color,
with replies to your support address, in the subscriber's `locale`, one of checkout's ten
languages ([White label](/white-label#emails-in-your-brand) has the details). The dashboard's
subscription settings preview each email in each language. Send `"emails": false` to send your own
from the webhooks instead.

## Cancellation and plan changes

- **You cancel** at once with `POST /v1/subscriptions/{id}/cancel`, which voids its open invoice, or
  at the end of the period with `PATCH` and `"cancel_at_period_end": true`. Nothing is refunded
  automatically: [refund a payment](/refunds) if you need to.
- **The subscriber cancels** from their page, at the end of the period, and can undo it until then.
- **With nothing paid for the current period** (`incomplete`, `past_due` or `unpaid`), a
  cancellation at the end of the period takes effect at once.
- **A plan change** (`PATCH` with `plan`) applies from the next period, with no proration.

## Webhooks

| Event                                                                                      | When                                                   |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| `subscription.created`                                                                     | Made, `trialing` or `incomplete`                       |
| `subscription.active`, `.past_due`, `.unpaid`, `.canceled`                                 | Each change to that status                             |
| `subscription.renewed`                                                                     | A new period began, paid for                           |
| `subscription.updated`                                                                     | The plan, cancellation at period end, subscriber or emails changed |
| `invoice.created`, `invoice.paid`, `invoice.void`, `invoice.reopened`                      | An invoice's changes                                   |

`data.object` is the object as `GET` shows it. Subscribe your endpoints to them by name. An
invoice's payments send their usual payment events, with `subscription` and `invoice`.

## In the dashboard

The dashboard's **Subscriptions** section lists your subscriptions, plans and invoices, with each
subscription's history, and its settings set the grace period, dunning and reminders. Members see it
with the team's `subscriptions` permission: to view, or to manage plans and subscriptions.

## In the sandbox

Walk a subscription through a year in a few calls:

- `POST /v1/sandbox/subscriptions/{id}/simulate` with `{"action":"advance","to":"next"}` moves its
  timeline so the next moment on its schedule is now (`invoice`, `due`, `grace_end`, `dunning_end`
  or `period_end` name one), and runs it.
- `POST /v1/sandbox/invoices/{id}/simulate` with `{"action":"pay"}` pays an invoice's remaining
  amount through a simulated deposit.

Live refuses both.
