title: Rates
description: Rates, estimates and minimums for prices in USD and EUR.
---

Rates come from Nowgate's own rate engine: Chainlink and Pyth feeds and exchange pools on the
networks Nowgate follows, every read checked by a second operator. An asset has a rate only when
two independent families of sources agree, one of them an oracle. See
[prices in a currency](/prices-and-rates).
