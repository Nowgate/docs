---
title: Sandbox
description: Simulate what payers do without testnet coins, and payouts without a chain.
---

In the sandbox, every case a payer can cause is one call away: paying, paying too little or too
much, paying again, sending the wrong asset, paying late, and running out of time. See
[testing in the sandbox](/payments#testing-in-the-sandbox).

Payouts and refunds in the sandbox are simulated too. They go through the same API, limits,
reservations, statuses and webhooks as live ones, but no chain: a payout is `sending` at once,
with a `sim:` transaction hash, and `finished` about 10 seconds later. Its network fee is a fixed
notional amount. The sandbox's balance is its simulated deposits, so a balance that came from a
real testnet deposit can't be paid out there: such a payout fails with
`simulated_funds_insufficient`. A new destination waits 5 minutes, or activate it at once.
