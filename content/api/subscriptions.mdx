---
title: Subscriptions
description: Plans, subscriptions and their invoices, each period paid on-chain as an ordinary payment.
---

A plan is what a subscription costs and how often. A subscription bills one email address on a
plan, with an invoice each period; each invoice is paid by ordinary [payments](/api/payments)
that carry its `invoice` and `subscription`. Nothing is charged automatically: Nowgate emails the
subscriber their invoices, reminders and notices, and moves the subscription through `past_due`,
`unpaid` and, at the end of dunning, `canceled`.
