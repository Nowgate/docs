---
title: Payouts
description: Pay your balance out to your own wallets, singly or in batches, to destinations that wait before their first use.
---

A payout pays an asset you hold to one of your **destinations**. A new destination waits before it
can be paid: 24 hours in live, 5 minutes in the sandbox. Its amount, Nowgate's payout fee and the
network fee, quoted in the payout's own asset, are reserved from your available balance as soon as
the payout is created. A payout that is canceled, or that fails, returns them, and a finished
payout returns what the network didn't take of its fee.

**Forwarding** pays what arrives on by itself: a rule per asset sends each payment's credit, or
your whole available balance once a day, to one of your destinations, the network fee coming out
of it.

Every operation that moves money, or sets up its movement, needs a key with the `payouts` scope,
which is always bound to an IP allowlist.
