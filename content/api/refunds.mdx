---
title: Refunds
description: Return a payment to its payer, in whole or in parts, in its own asset, to an address they give.
---

A refund pays a payment's own asset back to its payer, from your available balance. You can refund
a finished payment, or a partially paid one once it has closed, in one go or in parts, up to what it
credited (`amount_credited`). Nowgate keeps its payment fee, so a full refund draws the whole
credited amount from your balance. Like a [payout](/api/payouts), a refund is reserved as soon as it
is created, and a refund that is canceled, or that fails, returns it.

The network fee comes out of the refund by default (`fee_paid_by: "payee"`). Send
`fee_paid_by: "merchant"` to pay it yourself.

If you know the payer's address, send it with the refund. Otherwise the refund waits in
`awaiting_address`, and its `refund_url` opens a page where the payer gives their address, once.
Either way the address becomes one of your destinations, paid within about a minute like any new
one.

The payment keeps account of its refunds: `refund_pending` is what refunds under way will return,
and `amount_refunded` what finished ones did. Once that reaches `amount_credited`, the payment is
`refunded` and `payment.refunded` is sent.

Creating and canceling refunds needs a key with the `payouts` scope.
