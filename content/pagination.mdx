---
title: Pagination
description: Lists are newest first and page by ID. Walk them one page at a time, or let the SDKs walk every page.
---

Every list (payments, payment links, payouts, payout destinations, batches and refunds) is newest
first and answers one page:

```json
{ "object": "list", "data": [{ "id": "pay_…" }, { "id": "pay_…" }], "has_more": true }
```

| Parameter | What |
| --- | --- |
| `limit` | Items per page: 20 by default, at most 100. |
| `starting_after` | An ID from the previous page: the page of older items after it. |
| `ending_before` | An ID: the page of newer items before it. |

Plus each list's filters, such as a payment's `status` or `order_id`. `has_more` says whether
there is another page. Pages are by ID, so items created while you walk neither repeat nor shift
your walk: newer items appear in a new walk.

## Every page

`listAll` (`list_all` in Python) walks every page for you, newest first, asking for 100 items at a
time unless you give a `limit`, and follows `starting_after` while `has_more` is true. It fetches
nothing until you iterate, stops when you do, and retries each page as any `GET`.
