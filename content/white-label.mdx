---
title: White label
description: Serve checkout on your own domain, and send your customers emails in your brand.
---

Your payers and subscribers can see your brand from start to finish: checkout on a name of your
own, such as `pay.example.com`, and emails with your name, logo and colors.

## Checkout on your own domain

Add a name under **Settings → Domains** in the dashboard (it takes the settings' manage permission,
and asks you to confirm it's you), then create the two DNS records it shows, at your DNS provider:

| Record                         | Value                                                                       |
| ------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `pay.example.com` `CNAME`      | `domains.nowgate.io`. At a zone's apex, which can't hold a CNAME, `A` and `AAAA` records with its addresses instead |
| `_nowgate.pay.example.com` `TXT` | `nowgate-verification=` and the token the dashboard shows                  |

Nowgate checks both records, asking two public DNS resolvers run by different operators, and both
must agree; **Check now** asks them at once, at most once a minute. Then Nowgate obtains a
certificate for the name through ACME and serves your checkout there.

| Status      | Meaning                                                                          |
| ----------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `pending`   | Waiting for the records: checked every 5 minutes for an hour, then hourly        |
| `verified`  | The records check out; waiting for a certificate                                 |
| `active`    | Served, with a certificate                                                       |
| `error`     | A certificate couldn't be obtained after its retries; try again                  |
| `suspended` | The records were lost for three days in a row; no longer served                  |
| `failed`    | Not verified within 7 days, or suspended for 30; try again                       |

- **Choose it for checkout links** (one name at most, and it asks you to confirm it's you), and your
  payments', links', invoices' and subscribers' pages use it: in
  `checkout_url` and `subscriber_url`, in webhooks and in emails. If the name stops working, new
  links fall back to Nowgate's own address, and links already sent keep working there.
- **Only your pages** are served on your name: another merchant's link answers "not found" there.
- **White label:** on your name, checkout shows your brand alone, without Nowgate's mark.
- **Limits:** 3 names. A name verified by another merchant can't be added.
- **Certificates** are renewed at two-thirds of their lifetime. Members who manage settings get an
  email when a name becomes active, and if it's suspended or its certificate can't be renewed.

## Emails in your brand

Receipts and subscribers' emails carry your brand:

- **Your name** as the sender's: "Lumen Coffee via Nowgate", from Nowgate's address, so the email
  is authenticated as Nowgate sends it.
- **Replies** go to your support email.
- **Your logo**, attached to the email itself, so it shows even where remote images are blocked,
  and your **accent color**, with a text color that keeps it readable.
- **The footer** says who sent it and why.

Set your display name, logo, color and support email in the dashboard's general settings.
**Settings → Emails** shows what your emails carry and previews a receipt in each of checkout's ten
languages; the subscriptions' settings preview every email subscribers get.
