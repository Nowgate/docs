A payment is priced in an asset (`amount: "25"` USDT) or in a currency. For a currency, give
`price_amount` and `price_currency` (`USD` or `EUR`) instead of `amount`, and Nowgate converts the
price at its newest rate when it creates the payment. The payment's `amount` is then what the payer
sends, and `quote` shows the rate it was converted at.

```bash
curl https://api.nowgate.io/v1/payments \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $NOWGATE_API_KEY" \
  -H "Idempotency-Key: order-1043" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"network": "tron", "asset": "USDT", "price_amount": "49.00", "price_currency": "USD", "order_id": "1043"}'
```

In the SDKs, the same fields go to `payments.create`, as in [Payments](/payments). A price has at
most two decimal places, and is a string like every amount.

## How the amount holds

- **Until the payment expires:** 20 minutes by default for a price in a currency, an hour at most.
- **Late money is valued again.** Funds that arrive after expiry count for their value at the rate
  when Nowgate first saw them, so a payer can't take a quote and pay when the market suits them.
  `amount_counted` shows what the confirmed funds count for; your balance is credited with every
  unit that arrived either way.
- **Markup:** `markup_bps` (up to 1,000) adds to the converted amount, for example to cover
  volatility.
- **The fee:** with `fee_paid_by: "payer"`, the payer sends enough for you to receive the whole
  price after Nowgate's fee and the network costs. It works for prices in an asset too.

## Rates, estimates and minimums

<SdkTabs region="rates" />

- **`GET /v1/rates`** lists every asset's rate in USD and EUR, when it was taken, and whether it can
  be used. An asset without a usable rate says why in `reason`.
- **`GET /v1/estimate`** returns exactly the amount a payment created now would ask for, with its
  markup and fee, without holding anything.
- **`GET /v1/min-amount`** returns an asset's minimum payment, and with `currency` its value in that
  currency, so you can tell a customer the smallest price you can take in it.

## When there is no rate

Nowgate takes its rates from several sources of its own and uses one only when they agree. When no
rate can be trusted (the sources disagree, too few are fresh, a price jumped, or a stablecoin left
its peg), creation fails with [`rate_unavailable`](/errors/rate_unavailable). Retry with the same
`Idempotency-Key`, offer another asset, or price the payment in the asset.
