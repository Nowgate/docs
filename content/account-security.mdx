The dashboard is where you and your team manage payments, payouts, keys and settings. Every
account signs in with an email address and a password and, once you turn it on, a second step: a
code from your phone or a passkey. The actions that move money or open access ask you to confirm
it's you again.

## Two dashboards

| Dashboard | Address                          | Accounts                                    |
| --------- | -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Live      | `https://account.nowgate.io`     | Live accounts, live keys and payments       |
| Sandbox   | `https://sandbox-app.nowgate.io` | Sandbox accounts, sandbox keys and payments |

Each [environment](/#environments) has its own dashboard and its own accounts: nothing about a
sandbox account, session or key can reach live. Sign up in each one you use. The sandbox shows a
"Sandbox" banner on every page.

## Signing up

Sign up with your name, your email address and a password. Open the link in the email Nowgate
sends to verify your address: the link works for 24 hours, and you can't sign in until the address
is verified. The page you signed up on goes on by itself once the link is opened, here or on your
phone.

**Joining someone else's business?** Ask its owner for an [invitation](/team#invitations).

## Account Home and your businesses

Signing in takes you to **Account Home**: every business you belong to, with its logo, its name and
your role in it. Open one to work on it. The business switcher at the top of the sidebar moves to
another business, or back to Account Home.

- **Make a business** on Account Home: give it a name and, if you like, a logo (a PNG or JPEG,
  which the dashboard fits into a square). You're its owner. Its name and logo show at the top of
  its dashboard, on its checkout pages and in its emails, and you can change both in its settings.
- **Each business is its own:** its payments, balances, payment links, API keys, webhooks, team and
  settings. An account can own up to 25 businesses.
- **A new business is active at once,** in live as in the sandbox.

The first time you sign in, a short guided tour shows you around Account Home and your first
business. Skip it whenever you like; it doesn't come back.

## Passwords

- **At least 12 characters,** and at most 128. A long phrase is easiest to remember. There are no
  rules about digits or symbols, and passwords never expire.
- **Refused:** the 10,000 most common passwords of that length, a single repeated character, and
  your email address or its first part. The refusal says why.
- **Stored** as an argon2id hash: Nowgate never keeps your password itself.
- **Changing it** needs your current password, and asks you to
  [confirm it's you](#sensitive-actions-ask-again). It signs out your other sessions.
- **Forgot it?** Ask for a reset link on the sign-in page. It works once, for an hour, and setting a
  new password signs out every session. If you've turned on two-step sign-in, your next sign-in
  still asks for it: a reset never skips it.

## Two-step sign-in

Two-step sign-in is optional, and recommended: with it on, a stolen password alone can't reach
your account or your money. Turn it on from the **Account** page. You can hold several factors at
once, and should, so that losing a phone doesn't lock you out.

| Factor                     | What it is                                                                                                                      |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Authenticator app (TOTP)   | A six-digit code that changes every 30 seconds. Scan the QR code, then type a first code to confirm it. Each code works once. |
| Passkeys and security keys | Your device or key confirms it's you, with a fingerprint, face or PIN. Add as many as you like, and name each one.             |
| Recovery codes             | Ten codes of 16 characters, shown once with your first factor. Each works once, in place of a code. Save them somewhere safe.  |

Codes are typed one digit per box, and a whole code goes at once: paste it, or let your phone fill
it in. On the **Account** page you can add and remove factors, regenerate your recovery codes, and
change your password. Removing your last factor turns two-step sign-in off, and your recovery codes
with it.

**[Approving payouts](/approvals#approving)** always needs a factor of your own: an approver turns
two-step sign-in on first.

**Lost every factor?** Contact Nowgate support. Once they have confirmed it's you, they reset your
factors, and you sign in with your password.

## Sensitive actions ask again

Some actions need you to have confirmed it's you in the last 5 minutes: with a code or your
passkey, or, without two-step sign-in, with your password. When your last confirmation is older,
the dashboard asks, then carries on:

- changing your password, adding or removing a factor, regenerating recovery codes
- creating an API key; adding or changing a webhook endpoint, or rotating its secret
- adding a payout destination; creating a payout or a batch; setting up forwarding; creating a
  refund; returning a stuck deposit
- turning payout approvals off
- inviting a member, changing a member's permissions, removing a member, transferring ownership,
  setting a cashier's PIN
- creating a till, or giving it a new pairing code

**Approving payouts** always needs its own check, never a recent one. See
[approvals](/approvals#approving).

## Sessions

- **A session** ends after 7 days without activity, and after 30 days in any case. The dashboard
  then returns you to the sign-in page, and back to where you were.
- **The Account page** lists your active sessions with their browser, address and last activity.
  Sign out any of them, or every one but this.
- **Sessions live in a cookie** bound to the dashboard's own address, sent only with the dashboard's
  own requests, and unreadable to scripts. Every change also carries a token the page holds, so
  another site can't act in your name.
- **Removed from a team,** a member loses access to that business at once, on every session.

## Throttles and locks

Nowgate never says whether an address has an account: a wrong password and an unknown address get
the same answer. Repeated failures slow down whoever is guessing:

| What                             | Limit                                          | What happens                                                                                        |
| -------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Wrong passwords for one account  | 10 in 15 minutes                               | Password sign-in to that account is locked for 15 minutes, doubling with each lock up to 24 hours. A password reset ends the lock. |
| Wrong codes in one sign-in       | 5                                              | The sign-in starts again.                                                                           |
| Wrong codes for one account      | 10 in an hour                                  | The second step, and confirming it's you, are paused for an hour.                                   |
| Failed sign-ins from one address | 30 an hour                                     | That address waits (`429`).                                                                         |
| Sign-ups from one address        | 10 an hour                                     | That address waits (`429`).                                                                         |
| Reset links asked for            | 10 an hour per address, 3 an hour per account  | Beyond either, no email is sent. The page says the same either way.                                 |

A lock is never announced on the sign-in page. If you can't sign in with the right password, wait,
or reset your password.

## What you get by email

Nowgate emails only what you need:

- **Signing up:** a welcome, with the link that verifies your address. If someone signs up with an
  address that already has an account, its holder gets a note instead.
- **A password reset** link, when you ask for one, and **an invitation** to join a team.
- **A business made:** when you make a business.
- **A payment received** and **a payout sent:** to each business's owner and admins.

Links in these emails carry their token after a `#`, which browsers never send to servers, so it
stays out of every log.
