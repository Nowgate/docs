```bash
pip install nowgate
```

Its source is open, under the MIT license: [github.com/Nowgate/nowgate-python](https://github.com/Nowgate/nowgate-python).

The SDK needs Python 3.10 or later and nothing outside the standard library. It is fully typed
(`py.typed`): every result is a `TypedDict`, checked by mypy and your editor.

## Configure

<Sample sdk="python" region="client" />

| Option | Default | What |
|---|---|---|
| `api_key` | (needed) | Your API key. Its environment picks the API. |
| `base_url` | from the key | Another API, such as a local engine. |
| `timeout` | `60.0` | Seconds each attempt may wait for the network. |
| `max_retries` | `2` | How many times a failed attempt is retried. |
| `transport` | urllib | Your own HTTP client: see below. |
| `app_info` | none | Your application, added to the `User-Agent`: `my-shop/1.2`. |

Every method also takes `timeout=` and `max_retries=`, and every POST `idempotency_key=`.

## Errors

<Sample sdk="python" region="invalid-request" />

The exceptions are `NowgateError`, `ApiError` and its classes by status
(`AuthenticationError`, `PermissionDeniedError`, `NotFoundError`, `ConflictError`,
`InvalidRequestError`, `RateLimitError`, `ServerError`), `APIConnectionError` when there's no
answer, and `SignatureVerificationError`. See [Errors](/errors).

## Pages

<Sample sdk="python" region="list-all" />

`list_all` is a generator: it fetches nothing until you iterate, and stops when you do.

## Amounts

Amount fields take a decimal string, an `int` or a finite `decimal.Decimal`. A `float` is
refused with a `TypeError` before anything is sent. `Amounts.to_units`, `Amounts.from_units` and
`Amounts.compare` convert and compare amounts exactly. See [Amounts](/amounts).

## Webhooks

<Sample sdk="python" region="verify-webhook" />

`Webhook.verify_signature` and `Webhook.construct_event` take the raw body as `bytes` or `str`:
in Flask, `request.get_data()`; in Django, `request.body`. See [Webhooks](/webhooks).
