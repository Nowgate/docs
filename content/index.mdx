<Callout title="Start in the sandbox">
  [Open the sandbox](https://sandbox-app.nowgate.io/sign-up): the same engine as live, without
  real money. It is a system of its own, with accounts and keys separate from your main
  account's, and everything you build there works the same with real payments.
</Callout>

Nowgate gives each order its own deposit address on Bitcoin, Ethereum, BNB Smart Chain, Polygon,
Base, TRON, Solana or Bittensor. It watches the chain, waits until the deposit is final,
credits your balance and tells your server with a signed webhook.

## Networks and assets

Each network–asset pair is its own asset: USDT on TRON and USDT on Ethereum never share a
balance. Send both `network` and `asset` when you create a payment.

## How it fits together

- **Payment.** You create one per order with a network, an asset and an amount. It holds the
  deposit address you show your customer, and its status tells you where things stand.
- **Deposit.** Your customer sends funds to that address. Nowgate follows the chain, counts
  confirmations and handles reorganizations before anything is credited.
- **Webhook.** Every change to the payment is sent to your server as a signed event, retried for
  about 72 hours until you acknowledge it.
- **Balance.** Confirmed funds are credited to your balance in a double-entry ledger, minus
  Nowgate's fee.
- **Checkout.** Every payment has a [hosted checkout page](/checkout) at its `checkout_url`,
  and a [payment link](/payment-links) is a reusable one. Price either in an asset or in
  USD or EUR, and bring checkout to your own site with [the widget or the overlay](/embeds).
- **Dashboard.** Your team manages payments, payouts, keys and webhooks in the dashboard, each
  member with [their own permissions](/team) and [two-step sign-in](/account-security), and sells
  in person with [the point of sale](/point-of-sale).

## Environments

| Environment | Base URL                              | API keys          |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| Live        | `https://api.nowgate.io/v1`           | `ngk_live_…`      |
| Sandbox     | `https://sandbox-api.nowgate.io/v1`   | `ngk_sandbox_…`   |

The sandbox runs on test networks with its own keys and data. A key works only in its own
environment. It can also [play the payer](/payments#testing-in-the-sandbox), so you can test
every case without testnet coins.
