A [payment link](/api/payment-links) is a reusable checkout page that never expires, for a product,
a ticket or donations. Each attempt on it is an ordinary payment with `payment_link` set and its own
quote, and you get its events like any payment's.

<SdkTabs region="payment-link" />

Share the link's `checkout_url`, put it behind a [button](/embeds#buttons), or open it in
[the overlay](/embeds#the-overlay). Anyone with the URL can pay on the link.

## Prices

- **Fixed in a currency:** `price_amount` and `price_currency`; the payer picks any of the
  [coins you accept](/accepted-coins). Each attempt gets its own quote, as in
  [Prices and rates](/prices-and-rates).
- **Fixed in one asset:** `amount`, `network` and `asset`, as above.
- **Open:** up to six preset amounts, and optionally an amount the payer enters between a minimum
  and a maximum.

A link has no list of coins of its own: it follows your business's, so a change there reaches
every link at once, the ones already shared too. A request's `assets` is still taken and changes
nothing; an answer's `assets` is what a payer may choose now.

`markup_bps`, `fee_paid_by` and `expires_in` work as on a payment, for each attempt. Each attempt's
`order_id` can be numbered from `order_id_prefix`: `DONATE-1`, `DONATE-2` and so on.

## Donations

Checkout asks payers for the coin and the amount alone, so a donation carries no name and no
message. A link's `donations` settings stay in the API, and checkout no longer shows their fields.
A payment made before 18 November 2026 may still have a `donor`, plain text as entered: escape it
wherever you show it.

## Branding

A display name, an accent color (checkout picks a text color that meets WCAG AA on it) and a logo
you upload as a PNG or JPEG with [`POST /v1/branding/logos`](/api/payment-links#upload-a-logo)
(`logos.create` in the SDKs). After payment, checkout can offer a button back to your `success_url`;
it never redirects on its own.

## Changing a link

<SdkTabs region="update-payment-link" />

A new price applies to new attempts only. Set a link `inactive` to stop new attempts; its payments
still complete.
