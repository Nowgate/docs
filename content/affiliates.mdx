---
title: Affiliate program
description: Refer merchants to Nowgate and earn a share of the fees they pay, paid out like any balance.
---

Any active business can join the affiliate program from the dashboard's **Referrals** page (with
the team's `referrals` permission to manage). When a business you refer signs up, you earn a share
of Nowgate's payment fees on its payments, for a year.

## Referral links

Joining gives you a referral code. Share either link:

- `https://nowgate.io/?ref=<code>`: the site keeps the code for 30 days, and the last link followed
  wins.
- `https://account.nowgate.io/sign-up?ref=<code>`: straight to sign-up.

Add codes of your own to tell campaigns apart: 3 to 32 characters of `a-z`, `0-9` and `-`, up to 10
active, each with a label. A code can be turned off, and isn't reused.

A business is referred once: an account's first business, by the code its sign-up came with, from
the sign-up link or the site's cookie. A code that is unknown or turned off refers nobody, and the
sign-up goes on.

## Your share

- **20 % of Nowgate's payment fees** from each merchant you refer, for **365 days** from their
  sign-up. The terms are fixed when they sign up.
- **Only final fees count:** at once on networks with finality, and once a Bitcoin payment is
  settled. Payout fees and network fees aren't shared.
- **Held for 30 days**, then added to your available balance, in the coin the fee was paid in.
  Pay it out from the payouts pages or the API, like any balance.

## What you see

The affiliate program's page shows your links and codes with each code's sign-ups, the merchants
you referred (numbered, never named: a referral doesn't make their business yours), and your shares
by coin: held, released and voided, with each one's date and release date.
