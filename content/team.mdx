title: Your team

A business in the dashboard has one owner and any number of members. Each member's permissions
are set per section of the dashboard.

## Members

A member has either or both of:

- **a dashboard login:** an account of their own, with [two-step sign-in](/account-security#two-step-sign-in) if they turn it on
- **a till PIN:** to sell on your [point of sale](/point-of-sale)

A cashier who never uses the dashboard needs no email address: add them on the **Team** page with
a name and a PIN. One person can be a member of several businesses, and switches between them on
[Account home](/account-security#account-home-and-your-businesses) or at the top of the dashboard's
menu.

## Sections and permissions

Each member has a level per section: none, view or manage.

| Section      | View                                                         | Manage adds                                                         |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Payments     | Payments, their transfers and events; CSV export; stuck deposits | Accepting and returning [stuck deposits](/stuck-deposits)       |
| Links        | Payment links                                                | Creating, changing and archiving links; logos                       |
| Payouts      | Balances, the address book and the payouts' history          | Paying out; saving addresses; canceling a payout                    |
| Refunds      | Refunds                                                      | Creating and canceling refunds                                      |
| Developers   | API keys' details; webhook endpoints, events and deliveries  | Keys, endpoints, secrets, replays and test events                   |
| Settings     | The business's profile, branding, coins, receipts and defaults | Changing them                                                     |
| Team         | Members and invitations                                      | Inviting, changing, removing; cashiers and their PINs               |
| POS          | Tills and POS reports                                        | Creating, pairing, renaming and revoking tills; POS settings        |

One permission stands apart: **Sell** lets a member with a PIN sign in on your tills.

A member who manages payouts can pay out any amount of the balance, to any address: nobody else
approves a payout. Give that level only to people you would trust with the business's wallet.

## Roles

A role fills in the permissions. Change any of them one by one and the member's role becomes
`custom`.

| Role      | Payments | Links  | Payouts | Refunds | Developers | Settings | Team   | POS    | Sell |
| --------- | -------- | ------ | ------- | ------- | ---------- | -------- | ------ | ------ | ---- |
| owner     | manage   | manage | manage  | manage  | manage     | manage   | manage | manage | yes  |
| admin     | manage   | manage | manage  | manage  | manage     | manage   | manage | manage | yes  |
| finance   | view     | view   | manage  | manage  | none       | view     | none   | view   | no   |
| developer | view     | manage | view    | none    | manage     | view     | none   | none   | no   |
| support   | manage   | view   | view    | manage  | none       | none     | none   | view   | no   |
| viewer    | view     | view   | view    | view    | view       | view     | view   | view   | no   |
| cashier   | none     | none   | none    | none    | none       | none     | none   | none   | yes  |

- Nobody can give permissions they don't hold themselves.
- An admin can't change the owner's permissions.
- A member who opens a page beyond their permissions is told so, and the dashboard's menu shows
  only the sections they can see.

## Invitations

A member who manages the team invites an email address with a role. Inviting, like every change to
a member's permissions, asks you to [confirm it's you](/account-security#sensitive-actions-ask-again).

- **The link** in the invitation works once, for 7 days, and only for the address it was sent to.
- **Accepting** takes the invitee through sign-up if the address has no account yet, or signs them
  in if it has. Either way, their email is verified before they join.
- **Pending invitations** are listed on the Team page, where you can revoke one. Inviting the same
  address again replaces its pending invitation with a new link.
- Inviting someone who is already a member is refused.

## Removing a member

Removing a member takes effect at once: their sessions lose access to your business, and a
cashier's shift on a till ends. The owner can't be removed, only replaced.

## Ownership

Every business has exactly one owner. Only the owner can hand ownership over, to a member with a
dashboard login, and it asks you to confirm it's you. The new owner gets the owner role, and the
former owner becomes an admin.

Only the owner can delete the business, from
[Account home](/account-security#account-home-and-your-businesses).

## Cashiers and PINs

- **A PIN** is six digits. A member who manages the team sets it, confirming it's them, or the
  cashier changes it on a till after signing in, with their current one.
- **Any member can have one,** owners included, so whoever is at the counter can sell. Only members
  with the sell permission appear on your tills, by name and nothing more.
- **Guessing is throttled** on each till: see [the point of sale](/point-of-sale#cashiers-and-shifts).

## Notices

Nowgate emails the owner and admins of each business when it **receives a payment** and when a
**payout is sent**, and the owner when they **make a business**. Each email links to the page in
the dashboard; a link never acts by itself, because mail scanners follow links. The members who
manage payouts are also emailed when a payout goes to an address the business hasn't paid before,
or one is saved to the address book.
