## Statuses

| Status           | Meaning                                                                                             |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `waiting`        | Created; nothing seen on-chain yet.                                                                 |
| `confirming`     | A deposit is on-chain but not final yet. If a reorganization drops it, the payment returns to `waiting`. |
| `partially_paid` | Less than the amount has been confirmed. The customer can top up until the payment expires.         |
| `finished`       | The amount is confirmed and your balance is credited. Overpayments are credited in full and flagged. |
| `expired`        | Nothing arrived before the payment expired.                                                         |
| `failed`         | It can't be settled automatically, for example because only the wrong asset arrived. Nowgate reviews it. |
| `refunded`       | [Refunds](/api/refunds) returned everything it credited to the payer.                               |

Nowgate recomputes the status from what is on-chain every time something changes, so it never
drifts from the facts. Each change sends a [webhook](/webhooks).

## Amounts

Amounts are decimal strings in the asset's own units: `"49.90"` USDT, `"0.0015"` BTC. They are never
JSON numbers, and amounts with more decimal places than the asset has are rejected, not rounded.
Nowgate returns amounts in canonical form, so `"49.90"` comes back as `"49.9"`.

Every asset has a minimum payment so network fees can't eat it. The [network list](https://nowgate.io/networks)
and `GET /v1/assets` show each one.

| Field             | Meaning                                                    |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| `amount`          | What you asked for.                                        |
| `amount_pending`  | Seen on-chain, not final yet.                              |
| `amount_received` | Confirmed so far.                                          |
| `amount_credited` | Credited to your balance, before fees.                     |
| `amount_refunded` | Returned to the payer by finished refunds.                 |
| `refund_pending`  | What refunds under way will return.                        |
| `fee`             | Nowgate's fee on the credited amount.                      |
| `network_fee`     | Network costs, taken once from the credit.                 |

## Prices in a currency

Give `price_amount` and `price_currency` (`USD` or `EUR`) instead of `amount`, and Nowgate converts
the price at its newest rate when it creates the payment, with `quote` showing the rate. How long
the amount holds, markups, who pays the fee, and the rates, estimates and minimums behind it are in
[Prices and rates](/prices-and-rates).

## Payment links

A payment link is a reusable checkout page that never expires, for a product, a ticket or
donations; each attempt on it is an ordinary payment with `payment_link` set. See
[Payment links](/payment-links).

## Expiry

A payment expires after `expires_in` seconds (900 to 86,400; your account's default, usually an
hour, if you leave it out). A deposit counts as on time if Nowgate saw it before `expires_at`;
block timestamps are never used, because miners control them. A payment whose on-time deposit is
still confirming at expiry waits for it.

## Underpayments

If the customer sends less than the amount, the payment becomes `partially_paid` and they can top
up until it expires. At expiry, whatever arrived is credited and the payment stays
`partially_paid`. Decide yourself whether to deliver, refund or ask for the rest.

## Overpayments

If more arrives than the amount, the payment finishes, the whole amount received is credited, and
`overpaid` is `true`.

## Late payments

Funds that arrive after a payment expired are still yours: the payment reopens, the funds are
credited, and `late` becomes `true`. Nowgate never ignores customer funds.

## Confirmations and reorganizations

Nowgate credits a deposit only when its network's rule says it is final. Bitcoin waits for more
confirmations as amounts grow. Base credits at its safe head, once the block's data is posted to
Ethereum, and the other networks wait for their finalized block. The tier comes from the larger
of the deposit and the payment amount, so splitting a payment into small transfers can't lower
it.

If a reorganization drops a deposit that was already credited, the credit is reversed, `reversed`
becomes `true`, and you get a `payment.reversed` event.

## Testing in the sandbox

The sandbox (`sandbox-api.nowgate.io`) behaves as live does, with one addition: you can make up
what a payer does, with [`POST /v1/sandbox/payments/{id}/simulate`](/api/sandbox), and test every
case without testnet coins.

| `action` | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| `pay` | a deposit of exactly what is due |
| `underpay` | a deposit short by `amount`, or by half: `partially_paid` |
| `overpay` | a deposit over by `amount`, or by half: finished and `overpaid` |
| `repeat` | another deposit after the payment finished |
| `wrong_asset` | another asset of the network to the payment's address, which goes to review |
| `late` | the payment expires now, then what was due arrives: `late` |
| `expire` | the payment expires now |

A simulated deposit is pending at first, as one in the mempool is, and settles about ten seconds
later. From then on it is processed like any deposit, with the same statuses, credits and
webhooks. Simulated money stays in the sandbox's books and is never swept. In live the route
doesn't exist. See [the sandbox](/sandbox) for payouts and refunds there too.

## Retries and idempotency

Every `POST` needs an `Idempotency-Key` header. Sending the same request with the same key returns
the original response, with `Idempotent-Replayed: true`, instead of creating a second payment. The
same key with a different request is refused with
[`idempotency_key_reused`](/errors/idempotency_key_reused). Failed requests are not stored, so you
can retry them with the same key. See [Idempotency and retries](/idempotency) for what the SDKs
retry, and when.

## Listing payments

<SdkTabs region="list" />

`GET /v1/payments` returns payments newest first, 20 per page by default and up to 100. Filter by
`status` or `order_id`, and page with `starting_after` (older) or `ending_before` (newer) set to a
payment ID from the previous page. `has_more` says whether there is another page. See
[Pagination](/pagination).
