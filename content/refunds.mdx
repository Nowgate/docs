---
title: Refunds
description: Return a payment to its payer, in whole or in parts, to an address they give on a page of their own.
---

A refund sends a payment's own asset back to its payer, from your available balance. You can
refund a finished payment, or a partially paid one once it has closed, in one go or in parts, up
to what it credited. Nowgate keeps its payment fee, so a full refund draws the whole credited
amount from your balance. The [API reference](/api/refunds) has every field.

<SdkTabs region="refund">

```bash
curl https://api.nowgate.io/v1/payments/pay_.../refunds \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $NOWGATE_PAYOUTS_KEY" \
  -H "Idempotency-Key: $(uuidgen)" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"amount":"25"}'
```

</SdkTabs>

## The payer's address

If you know the payer's address, send it with the refund. Otherwise the refund waits in
`awaiting_address`, and its `refund_url` opens a page where the payer gives their address, in
their language. Send them the link.

- **The address is given once.** A wrong one needs you to cancel the refund and refund again.
- **It becomes a destination**, paid within about a minute, like any new one.
- The page shows the payer what they will receive, when it will be sent, and then its transaction.

## What the payment shows

| Field             | Meaning                                         |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| `refund_pending`  | What refunds under way will return.             |
| `amount_refunded` | What finished refunds returned.                 |

Once finished refunds reach what the payment credited, it becomes `refunded` and
`payment.refunded` is sent. Anything the payer sends to it afterwards isn't credited to you.

## Fees, statuses and events

- **The network fee comes out of the refund** by default (`fee_paid_by: "payee"`); send
  `fee_paid_by: "merchant"` to pay it yourself.
- **A refund is a payout** of kind refund, with an `rf_` ID: the same statuses, limits and
  approvals. It sends `refund.created`, then `refund.<status>` on every change.
- **Cancel one** with `POST /v1/refunds/{id}/cancel` until it is signed; its hold comes back to
  your balance and to the payment.
- **Refused:** `not_refundable` when the payment isn't finished or closed, or its refunds already
  hold everything it credited, and `refund_exceeds_refundable` for more than remains.
