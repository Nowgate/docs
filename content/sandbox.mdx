---
title: Sandbox
description: The sandbox behaves as live does, without real money, and lets you play the payer, fail a payout or skip a destination's wait.
---

The sandbox (`https://sandbox-api.nowgate.io/v1`, with an `ngk_sandbox_…` key) runs the same
engine as live, with the same statuses, credits, webhooks, payouts and refunds, but no real
money: simulated deposits stay in the sandbox's books and payouts are never sent. It adds
[controls](/api/sandbox) that live doesn't have.

The sandbox is a system of its own. Its accounts, keys and data are separate from live's: an
account made at `signup.nowgate.io` doesn't sign in to the sandbox, and a sandbox account
doesn't sign in at `signin.nowgate.io`. [Create a sandbox account](https://sandbox-app.nowgate.io/sign-up)
to get its keys.

## Play the payer

<SdkTabs region="simulate-payment" />

| `action` | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| `pay` | a deposit of exactly what is due |
| `underpay` | a deposit short by `amount`, or by half: `partially_paid` |
| `overpay` | a deposit over by `amount`, or by half: finished and `overpaid` |
| `repeat` | another deposit after the payment finished |
| `wrong_asset` | another asset of the network to the payment's address, which goes to review |
| `late` | the payment expires now, then what was due arrives: `late` |
| `expire` | the payment expires now |

## Payouts and refunds

A new payout destination waits before its first payout, as in live, unless the sandbox activates
it at once. Payouts and refunds then run to `finished` with simulated transactions, and
`POST /v1/sandbox/payouts/{id}/simulate` can make one fail.
